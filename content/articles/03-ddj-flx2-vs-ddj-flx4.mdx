<Section id="quick-comparison" title="Quick comparison" />

Both controllers share the same 2-deck layout, performance pads, and rekordbox unlock. The differences are physical: the FLX4 is larger with bigger jog wheels, the FLX2 is smaller and lighter. On paper the specs are nearly identical — in the hand they feel like different instruments.

<Section id="who-should-buy" title="Who should buy which" />

<Note title="Buy the FLX4 if">You mix at home, want room to grow, and value the larger jog wheels and full-size layout. The FLX4 is the better long-term investment.</Note>

<Note title="Buy the FLX2 if">You gig out of a backpack, prioritize portability, and don't mind smaller jogs. The FLX2 is the genuinely portable sibling.</Note>

<Section id="verdict" title="Verdict" />

If you plan to gig out of a backpack, FLX2. If you mix at home or want room to grow, FLX4. The FLX4's larger jog wheels and proper layout make it the better learning instrument; the FLX2's 1.6kg weight makes it the better travel companion.
