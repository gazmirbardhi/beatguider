<Section id="before-you-plug-in" title="Before you plug anything in" />

The #1 cause of silence at mobile gigs is power, not gear. Before you plug a single thing in, verify the venue's electrical situation. A shared circuit with a fridge or a kettle will cause dropouts or worse.

<Caution title="Never assume">Never assume the venue has adequate power. Always ask about the circuit amperage, what else is on it, and whether there's a dedicated circuit for the DJ.</Caution>

<Section id="the-checklist" title="The gig-day checklist" />

<Checklist items={[{ id: "p1", text: "Confirm dedicated circuit — no shared fridge / kettle / stage lights" }, { id: "p2", text: "Verify RCD/GFCI protection on the outlet feeding your rig" }, { id: "p3", text: "Measure actual draw with a clamp meter before soundcheck" }, { id: "p4", text: "Carry 2× the cable length you think you need" }, { id: "p5", text: "Have a battery-powered backup for the ceremony / announcements" }, { id: "p6", text: "Label your power cables so you can trace them in the dark" }, { id: "p7", text: "Bring a surge protector — venue power is dirty" }]} />

<Section id="sizing" title="Sizing your rig" />

A typical mobile DJ rig (controller + 2 powered speakers + lights) draws 800-1500W continuous. Check each component's nameplate rating, add them up, then add 50% headroom for inrush. Use our Power Calculator tool to size your generator or verify the venue circuit.
