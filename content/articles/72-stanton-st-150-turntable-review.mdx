<Section id="faq" title="Frequently asked questions" />

### Is the Stanton ST-150 good for scratching?

Yes, the ST-150 is considered a scratcher's dream. Its powerful direct-drive motor (4.5kg torque) is more powerful than Technics 1200 motors and can handle heavy scratching, beat juggling, and abuse.

#### What is the difference between the ST-150 and STR8-150?

The ST-150 has an S-curved tonearm with height fine adjust and anti-skate, while the STR8-150 has a straight tonearm designed for scratching and mixing.

#### Does the Stanton ST-150 come with a dust cover?

No, the ST-150 does not come with a plastic dust cover. This is one of the few downsides of this turntable.

#### What software is included with the Stanton ST-150?

The ST-150 comes with a full version of Deckadance DVS software included in the box.
