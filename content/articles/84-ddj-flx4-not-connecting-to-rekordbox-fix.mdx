If rekordbox does not respond to your DDJ-FLX4, start with the physical USB connection and confirm whether the computer can see the controller. Do not install a third-party driver or reset software settings until you know which part of the connection is failing.

## Quick answer

Try these steps in order:

1. Connect the computer to the DDJ-FLX4's **USB-C device-connection port**, not its USB-C power-supply port.
2. Connect directly to the computer. Remove any hub, dock, monitor, or adapter chain for this test.
3. Use a known-good, data-capable USB cable. A cable can supply power while failing to carry usable data.
4. Quit rekordbox and any other DJ software. Disconnect the controller, reconnect it directly, wait for its startup illumination, and reopen rekordbox.
5. Check whether the controller appears in Windows Device Manager or macOS System Information.
6. If the operating system sees it, update rekordbox and check its audio device selection. If the operating system does not see it, keep testing the cable, port, and another computer.
7. Confirm the controller firmware is current. Follow AlphaTheta's updater instructions exactly and do not disconnect the cable during an update.

The result of step 5 determines which half of this guide you need.

| What you find | What it means | Go next |
|---|---|---|
| No startup lights | The controller may not be receiving enough power, or the cable, computer port, or controller may have a fault | Test the correct controller port, another data cable, another direct computer port, and the laptop's AC power |
| Lights come on, but the OS does not list DDJ-FLX4 | Power is present, but a working data connection has not been confirmed | Focus on the device-connection port, cable, hub removal, and a second computer |
| The OS lists DDJ-FLX4, but rekordbox does not respond | The physical USB link is working at the OS level | Restart the software, update rekordbox, and isolate other DJ applications |
| Controls work, but there is no master or headphone sound | The controller is connected | Skip to the audio-settings check |

## 1. Use the correct USB-C port on the controller

The DDJ-FLX4 has two rear USB-C ports with different jobs:

- The **device-connection USB port** carries data between the controller and a computer or mobile device.
- The **power-supply USB port** accepts external USB power for setups that need it.

For a PC or Mac connection, plug the computer into the device-connection port. AlphaTheta says an external power adapter or battery is not needed when the controller is connected to a computer; the computer supplies power through the device connection.

If the IN and OUT buttons on both decks flash and the controls do not work, AlphaTheta specifically directs computer users to disconnect any adapter or battery from the power-supply port and connect the computer to the device-connection port.

<Note title="The controller end is USB-C">

The DDJ-FLX4 does not use the square USB-B “printer cable” found on some older DJ controllers. A USB-B cable cannot connect to the DDJ-FLX4's USB-C device port.

</Note>

## 2. Test a known-good data cable

A lit controller proves that electrical power reaches it; it does not prove that the cable is carrying data correctly. USB-C cables vary in their capabilities, and a damaged or power-only cable can produce the “lights on, no control” symptom.

For a clean cable test:

1. Remove extensions, magnetic tips, and adapter chains.
2. Use the supplied cable if it is available and undamaged, or another cable known to transfer data with a different USB device.
3. Connect the cable directly between the computer and the controller's device-connection port.
4. Check the operating-system detection result before trying another change.
5. Repeat with a second known-good data cable if the controller is absent.

Do not test this cable by looking for the controller in Finder or File Explorer. The DDJ-FLX4 is not a storage drive and is not expected to mount there.

## 3. Remove hubs and docks

AlphaTheta recommends connecting the DDJ-FLX4 directly to a PC or Mac. Its support guidance says a USB hub can cause noise or malfunction; if a hub cannot be avoided, use one recommended by the computer manufacturer.

For diagnosis, remove the hub even if other USB devices work through it. Also disconnect nonessential USB devices temporarily. This gives you one controller, one cable, and one computer port to test.

If your laptop only has USB-C ports, use a direct USB-C data cable where possible. If an adapter is unavoidable, record its brand and model in your test notes because it remains part of the connection path.

## 4. Reconnect in a clean software state

Use this sequence to remove a stalled app session from the test:

1. Quit rekordbox completely.
2. Quit Serato DJ Lite, Serato DJ Pro, MIDI utilities, and any other DJ or audio-control software that is open.
3. Disconnect the DDJ-FLX4's device cable.
4. Reconnect it directly to the computer with the known-good cable.
5. Wait for the controller's startup illumination to settle.
6. Open the current version of rekordbox.

The DDJ-FLX4 has no separate power switch to use in this sequence. It receives power when the correct USB connection is made.

If the controller now works, reopen other applications one at a time later to identify whether one of them contributed to the conflict. Do not leave multiple DJ applications open during the baseline test.

## 5. Check whether the operating system detects the controller

This is the most useful branch in the diagnosis. If the OS cannot see the DDJ-FLX4, changing rekordbox audio preferences is unlikely to fix the underlying USB connection.

### Windows 10 or 11

1. Right-click **Start** and open **Device Manager**.
2. Expand **Sound, video and game controllers**.
3. Look for **DDJ-FLX4**.

For a firmware-version check, AlphaTheta's update guide uses **DDJ-FLX4 > Properties > Details > Hardware Ids**. The hardware ID includes a `REV_` value that the guide maps to the installed firmware version.

If DDJ-FLX4 is absent, keep Device Manager open while you disconnect and reconnect the controller. No change in the device list points back to the connection path: correct USB-C port, cable, computer port, or hardware.

### macOS

1. Open the Apple menu and choose **About This Mac**.
2. Open **System Report** (the exact route to this button varies slightly by macOS version).
3. Under **Hardware**, select **USB**.
4. Look for **DDJ-FLX4**.

AlphaTheta uses this same System Information view to check the installed firmware version on a Mac. You can also open **Audio MIDI Setup** and look for the controller in the audio-device sidebar, but System Information is the clearer first check for the USB link.

### How to use the result

- **Absent on one port only:** use a port where the controller is detected and have the failing port checked separately if other devices also fail there.
- **Absent with two data cables and every direct port:** test the same controller and cable on another supported computer.
- **Present on another computer only:** the first computer's USB path, operating system, or software state needs further isolation.
- **Absent on two supported computers with two known-good cables:** stop changing software settings and contact AlphaTheta support; the controller may need service.
- **Present in the OS:** move to the rekordbox and firmware checks below.

## 6. Update rekordbox and check OS support

At the time this page was verified (**August 9, 2026**), the current rekordbox release was **7.2.16**, released **July 9, 2026**. Use the [official rekordbox download and release-notes page](https://rekordbox.com/en/download/) to check the current version rather than treating the number here as permanent.

The same official page currently lists these desktop operating systems:

- Windows 11
- Windows 10 with the latest service pack
- macOS Tahoe 26, Sequoia 15, Sonoma 14, and Ventura 13, each updated to the latest point release

AlphaTheta notes that meeting the system requirements does not guarantee operation on every computer. On a laptop, connect its AC power adapter during troubleshooting, as recommended in AlphaTheta's DDJ-FLX4 software information.

If you are using an older rekordbox major version because of an established library or performance setup, back up the library before updating. The connection test should use a supported rekordbox version, but a software upgrade should not be treated as a substitute for a library backup.

## 7. Do not install a separate DDJ-FLX4 driver

The DDJ-FLX4 does **not** require a separate manufacturer driver on Windows or macOS. AlphaTheta says it uses the standard audio driver included with the operating system and that the driver installs automatically when the controller is connected.

That means these are not appropriate fixes for this model:

- downloading an unofficial “DDJ-FLX4 driver”;
- disabling Windows driver-signature enforcement;
- uninstalling unrelated ASIO drivers;
- changing macOS microphone, Bluetooth, or Full Disk Access permissions solely to make the controller appear as a USB device.

If Windows shows the controller in Device Manager, Windows has detected it. If it does not appear there, return to the cable-and-port branch rather than searching for a separate Pioneer DJ or AlphaTheta driver package.

## 8. Check and update DDJ-FLX4 firmware

At the time of verification, AlphaTheta listed **DDJ-FLX4 firmware 1.07**, released **September 4, 2025**, for Windows and macOS. Check the current [Windows firmware page](https://support.alphatheta.com/en-us/articles/12292065104537) or [Mac firmware page](https://support.alphatheta.com/en-us/articles/12292227210393) before downloading.

Firmware 1.07's published change concerns a Beat FX issue. AlphaTheta does not describe it as a general USB-recognition fix, so updating firmware should remove an outdated-version variable—not be presented as a guaranteed connection repair.

To check the installed version:

- **Windows:** In Device Manager, open **DDJ-FLX4 > Properties > Details > Hardware Ids** and compare its `REV_` value with AlphaTheta's update guide.
- **macOS:** In **System Information > Hardware > USB**, select DDJ-FLX4 and read its Version value.

Before updating, read the [official Firmware Update Guide](https://downloads.support.alphatheta.com/firmwares/dj-controllers/DDJ-FLX4/DDJ-FLX4_update_manual_e_3rd.pdf). Close all applications, connect the laptop to AC power, run the correct updater for the OS, and do not disconnect the USB cable or shut down the computer until the updater reports completion.

<Caution title="Do not interrupt a firmware update">

An interrupted firmware write can leave the controller unusable. If the controller is not detected by the updater, return to the direct cable and OS-detection checks rather than repeatedly starting the update.

</Caution>

## 9. If rekordbox sees the controller but there is no sound

If the jog wheels, transport controls, or mixer controls affect rekordbox, the connection problem is solved. Treat missing sound as a separate audio-routing problem.

Open rekordbox **Preferences > Audio** and confirm that DDJ-FLX4 is selected as the audio device. Then check:

- the master and headphone output assignments;
- channel input/gain, channel faders, and crossfader position;
- the master level;
- headphone cue buttons, cue/mix position, and headphone level;
- speaker and headphone connections to the controller.

Do not change sample rate, buffer size, or MIDI mappings at random. Record the existing settings first, then change one item at a time. The broader [DJ audio troubleshooting guide](/fix/dj-audio-troubleshooting-complete-guide) covers a controller that is connected but silent.

## 10. Run a two-cable, two-computer isolation test

If the earlier steps did not identify the fault, use a small test matrix. Keep everything except the named variable unchanged.

| Test | Result to record | What it helps isolate |
|---|---|---|
| Cable A, direct port 1 | Lights; OS detection; rekordbox response | Baseline |
| Cable A, direct port 2 | Same three observations | Computer port |
| Cable B, known-good data cable | Same three observations | Cable |
| Same controller and cable on computer B | Same three observations | First computer versus controller |

Keep blank fields for the exact hardware, software versions, steps, results, photos, and screenshots as you work through each test in the matrix. Those results are required before BeatGuider can describe any fix as physically reproduced.

## When to contact AlphaTheta support

Contact [AlphaTheta product support](https://support.alphatheta.com/en-us/products/12077079532697) when:

- the DDJ-FLX4 is absent on two supported computers using two known-good data cables;
- the controller shows no startup illumination on direct ports that power other devices;
- the firmware updater failed or the controller became unresponsive during an update;
- a USB connector is loose, damaged, or works only when the cable is held at an angle.

Include the controller serial number, computer model, exact OS version, rekordbox version, installed firmware version if readable, cables and ports tested, and screenshots of Device Manager or System Information. Clear test notes are more useful than a list of unrelated setting changes.

<Section id="faq" title="Frequently asked questions" />

### Why does my DDJ-FLX4 light up but not control rekordbox?

Lights confirm that the controller is receiving power, but they do not prove that its USB data connection is working. Connect the computer to the DDJ-FLX4 USB-C device-connection port, use a known-good data-capable cable, bypass hubs, and then check whether Windows or macOS detects the controller.

#### Does the DDJ-FLX4 need a driver on Windows or macOS?

No separate DDJ-FLX4 driver is required. AlphaTheta says the controller uses the standard audio driver included with Windows and macOS, which installs automatically when the controller is connected.

#### Which USB-C port should I use on the DDJ-FLX4?

Connect the computer to the rear USB-C port marked for device connection, not the separate USB-C power-supply port. When using a PC or Mac, AlphaTheta says external USB power is not required.

#### What are the current DDJ-FLX4 firmware and rekordbox versions?

When this guide was verified on August 9, 2026, the current DDJ-FLX4 firmware was 1.07, released September 4, 2025, and the current rekordbox release was 7.2.16, released July 9, 2026. Check the official download pages because these versions will change.

#### How can I tell whether my computer detects the DDJ-FLX4?

On Windows, look for DDJ-FLX4 under Sound, video and game controllers in Device Manager. On macOS, open System Information and look under Hardware > USB. If the controller is absent, continue testing the USB port and cable before changing rekordbox settings.

#### What if the DDJ-FLX4 is detected but there is no sound?

That is an audio-routing problem rather than a USB-recognition problem. In rekordbox Preferences, confirm that DDJ-FLX4 is the selected audio device, then check the master and headphone output assignments, channel trim, faders, and master level.
